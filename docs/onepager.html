MedTimeline — your family's health story, organized

DSH Hacks V2 · Theme: AI × Healthcare
Built by 十字架 maebahesioru · github.com/maebahesioru/medtimeline

Purpose

When you care for an aging parent or manage a chronic condition, medical documents arrive scattered: a lab report from March, a follow-up in September, a discharge summary from a hospital visit. No one shows you the story — that HbA1c is trending up, that ferritin is falling, that a value flagged HIGH in September was borderline in March. Caregivers become accidental data managers, with no tools built for them. MedTimeline turns scattered reports into one chronological health story — with trends, flagged values, and questions for the next appointment — and answers every question with citations to the exact line.

Key features

  • Multi-document ingestion — upload reports from any dates (PDF / TXT / MD); every passage keeps its exact origin.
  • Chronological timeline — reports and measurements assembled into one dated story across all documents.
  • Trend detection — repeated measurements compared across dates (e.g. "Ferritin: 22 ng/mL (2026-03) → 9 ng/mL (2026-09) — falling"), both values and dates shown.
  • Flagged values — LOW/HIGH results surfaced with their reference ranges, straight from the documents.
  • Appointment prep — auto-generated questions for the next visit, built from the trends and flags ("HbA1c rose from 5.6 to 5.9 — what could explain this?").
  • 🔊 Spoken questions — one button reads the appointment questions aloud.
  • Grounded Q&A — ask anything about the documents; answers cite the exact line and compare values across reports; off-document questions are declined.
Safety by design: MedTimeline is an organizer and translator, not a doctor. It never diagnoses, never prescribes, and does not interpret symptoms. Answers are restricted to retrieved excerpts of the uploaded documents; off-document questions return an explicit refusal. Appointment questions are phrased as questions for your clinician. The bundled sample reports are entirely synthetic (fictional patient data), and documents stay on the user's machine in this prototype.

How it works

Python + FastAPI backend; single-page web UI (no build step). Timeline extraction parses dated report rows into events, groups repeated measurements, and computes trends (rising/falling/stable) with source links. Retrieval is scikit-learn TF-IDF (local, any language); generation is retrieval-scoped with mandatory citations and an explicit refusal token. Audio uses edge-tts with content-hash caching. Any OpenAI-compatible LLM endpoint can be configured.

Impact

  • Caregivers — the person managing a parent's care finally sees the whole story.
  • Continuity — trends across providers and dates, which no single report shows.
  • Preparedness — arrive at appointments with specific, document-grounded questions.
  • Safety — grounded answers, honest refusals, and clinician handoff.
